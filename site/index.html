Open Campus

A free university, written in the open, for every major.

Under construction. There is nothing to study here yet. The first program is Accounting.

What it is

Open Campus is a library of university courses written as open data: readings, worked examples, practice questions, exercises and exams, for the majors that most students study. Anyone can use it, at no cost and without an account.

It is built by AI models working under rules, with people deciding what only people should decide. Each time a better model is released, the whole library is checked again.

How it is built

  1. Every course maps by code to an outside standard it does not write: a licensing exam, a credit-by-exam outline, or a professional curriculum guideline. Progress is measured somewhere we do not control.
  2. Every item carries a record of who wrote it and who checked it, and the checker is never the writer.
  3. Content is our own wording, or comes from sources whose licenses allow reuse. Paid prep material never comes in.
  4. Each course and program shows how finished it is. Nothing is presented as more complete than it is.
  5. Each new model release is a scheduled audit of the whole library, and the error rate it finds is published.

What it is not

Open Campus is not accredited, grants no degrees or credit, and is not affiliated with any testing or licensing body. Exam names describe what a course prepares for. Licensing exams have their own eligibility rules, such as accredited coursework or supervised hours, which Open Campus cannot satisfy.
